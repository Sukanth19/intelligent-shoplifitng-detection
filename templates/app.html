    Test-set performance

    Frame-level ROC-AUC and AP on the official test split (standard protocol for weakly supervised video anomaly detection). The checkpoint was selected on a validation split held out from the training videos.

    Training curve

    Pipeline

    1. Snippets 16 consecutive frames, resized to 224 px height
    2. Video Swin-B Kinetics-400 pretrained 3D transformer (same checkpoint as the training features) → 1024-d feature per snippet
    3. Multi-sequence windows overlapping windows of 32 snippets (stride 16)
    4. Transformer encoder self-attention over time inside each window
    5. Two heads snippet anomaly score + video-level anomaly probability
    6. Score correction snippet score × video probability

    Training (weak supervision)

    Only video-level labels (normal / anomalous) are used. The K-snippet sequence with the highest mean score must be high for anomalous and low for normal videos. Self-training shrinks K over four stages (32 → 16 → 8 → 4) and uses the previous stage's predictions as snippet pseudo-labels.
